# 🍕 Pizza Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an **interactive Power BI dashboard** built to analyze pizza sales performance. It provides a clear view of revenue, quantity sold, pricing trends, and category-wise performance to support **data-driven business decisions**.

The dashboard is designed with clean visuals, slicers, and KPIs to help users quickly understand sales patterns and customer preferences.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives

* Analyze **total sales and quantity** performance
* Understand **average and maximum unit prices**
* Compare sales across **pizza sizes and categories**
* Track **monthly sales trends**
* Enable interactive filtering for deeper insights

---

## 📌 Key KPIs

* **Total Price:** 817.86K
* **Total Quantity Sold:** 50K
* **Average Unit Price:** 16.49
* **Maximum Unit Price:** 35.95

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals

### 1. KPI Cards

Displays overall performance metrics:

* Total Price
* Total Quantity
* Average Unit Price
* Maximum Unit Price

### 2. Sales by Pizza Size (Bar Chart)

Shows total sales value by pizza size:

* L size contributes the highest revenue
* XXL and XL have minimal sales impact

### 3. Sales Distribution by Category (Pie Chart)

Compares sales among categories:

* Classic
* Supreme
* Veggie
* Chicken

### 4. Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)

* Tracks total sales across months
* Highlights peak and low-performing periods

### 5. Quantity by Pizza Category (Horizontal Bar Chart)

* Classic category leads in quantity sold
* Chicken and Veggie follow closely
